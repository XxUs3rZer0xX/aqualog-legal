Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Applies to the AQUALOG Android app (tech.us3rzer0.aqualog)

AQUALOG helps pool and spa service companies record water tests, chemical additions, and compliance logs. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

AQUALOG does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. It contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

§2How data is stored

  • Data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, on servers in the United States (AWS us-east-1).
  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Each company's data is isolated by database access rules. Members of one company cannot read another company's records.
  • The app keeps a local copy on your phone so you can log tests offline. It syncs when you reconnect and is removed when you delete your account.

§3Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. We share it only in these cases:

  • Report links you create. A company member can generate a read-only link to one facility's test and chemical history, for example to send to a health inspector or property manager. Anyone with the link can view that facility's report until a member revokes it. It does not reveal who took each reading.
  • Service providers. Supabase processes data on our behalf to run the app.
  • Legal requirements. If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety of people or the service.

§4Delete your account and data

In the app: open Account, choose Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately.

Without the app: email us from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my AQUALOG account". We complete web requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

support@us3rzer0.tech

What gets deleted:

  • Your login, email address, name, and profile are deleted.
  • If you are the last member of your company, the company and all of its facilities, water tests, chemical logs, and report links are deleted too.
  • If other members remain, the tests and chemical logs you recorded stay with the company as part of its compliance history, with your name removed. If you were the only admin, the longest-standing remaining member becomes admin.
  • Deleted data may remain in encrypted server backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

§5Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Water test records are often required for health department inspections, so we do not delete them automatically. Your company's admins decide what to keep, and you can delete your account at any time (§4).

§6Your choices

You can view and correct most of your data directly in the app. To request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, email the address in §4. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law; we honor those requests through the same address.

§7Children

AQUALOG is a business tool for pool and spa professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

§8Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Questions go to support@us3rzer0.tech.
